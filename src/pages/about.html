About carapr.org

Free calculators for car buyers and owners, built to make the cost of a car easier to see.

What this site is

carapr.org is an independent site with free calculators for people who are buying or owning a car. There is nothing to sign up for, and you never need to share your personal details.

  • Car APR calculator: finds the real rate on a loan from the payment, the amount, and the term.
  • Down payment savings calculator: shows how long it takes to save for a down payment.
  • Pay off early calculator: shows the interest you save by paying extra each month.
  • Refinance calculator: compares your loan to a new rate and term.
  • Negative equity calculator: compares what you owe to what the car is worth.
  • Gig driver profit calculator: shows real hourly pay after gas, car costs, and taxes.

Why the APR calculator exists

Car loans are often quoted as a monthly payment, and the payment alone hides how much the loan costs. Working backward from the payment to the rate shows what you are really paying, so you can compare offers on equal terms.

How the calculators work

Each calculator uses standard formulas, and each page explains the math in plain language. Everything runs in your browser, and the figures you enter are not sent to us. Results are estimates. Your lender's loan documents, your bank statements, and your tax professional are the official sources for your own numbers.

What we are not

carapr.org is not a lender, a broker, or a financial advisor. We do not offer loans, take applications, or recommend specific lenders. Nothing here is financial, legal, or tax advice.

How the site is paid for

The site shows ads, which cover hosting and the time it takes to build and update the calculators. Ads do not change the results the calculators give. See the privacy policy for how advertising works on this site.

Spot a mistake?

If a result looks wrong or a page is out of date, please tell us at the contact page. Corrections are welcome, and so are ideas for new calculators.

Results are estimates for information only and are not financial advice. Your lender's disclosed APR is the official figure.
