Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026. This policy explains what carapr.org collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

What happens to the numbers you enter

The calculators on carapr.org run in your web browser. The loan amounts, payments, mileage, and other figures you type in are not sent to our servers and are not stored by us. When you close the page, they are gone.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the service that hosts carapr.org may keep standard server logs. These can include your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you request, and the time of the request. We use this kind of information to run the site, keep it secure, and understand overall traffic.

Advertising and cookies

carapr.org shows ads from Google AdSense and may work with other advertising partners. These partners use cookies and similar technologies to show ads and to measure them.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to this site and other websites. Google's advertising cookies let Google and its partners show you ads based on your visits to this site and to other sites on the internet.

You can turn off personalized advertising from Google in Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of many other companies' personalized advertising at aboutads.info. To read how Google uses data from sites like this one, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland may see a message asking for consent before ads that use cookies are shown. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, although some ads and features may then work differently.

Your country setting

When you pick a country, your browser remembers it on your own device (in local storage) so the calculators open in that country next time. When you change it, Google Analytics records which country you picked, but never the numbers you enter. If you do not pick one, the site guesses from your browser’s language setting. Clear your browser’s site data to remove it.

Fonts

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests them, Google receives your IP address and basic browser information.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use carapr.org, such as which pages and calculators are used, which country setting is chosen, and roughly where visitors come from. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your IP address, device and browser type, and the pages you visit. It does not receive the numbers you type into the calculators.

You can learn how Google uses this data at How Google uses information from sites that use its services, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Links to other websites

Some pages link to other sites, such as the IRS. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Your privacy rights

We do not sell your personal information for money. Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act may give you rights to ask what information a business holds about you, to ask for it to be deleted, and to opt out of certain uses of it for advertising. Because we do not keep the numbers you enter, the information we hold is limited. To make a request, email contact@carapr.org.

Children

carapr.org is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to contact@carapr.org.

Results are estimates for information only and are not financial advice. Your lender's disclosed APR is the official figure.
